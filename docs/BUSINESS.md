# The business

## One line

AI memory is locked inside every vendor, the law now says it must move,
nobody owns the rails it moves on — we build them, starting as the movers,
becoming the pipes, then the standard all AI memory is written in.

## The problem (why anyone buys)

A company runs Copilot for employees, Agentforce for customers, Claude for
engineering, plus custom agents. Four AI brains that don't share what they
learn, each deepening its own lock-in, none owned by the company. When a
contract ends, that slice of accumulated knowledge dies. 94% of organizations
cite vendor lock-in as a top concern; the average enterprise already runs
12+ agents across several platforms.

## Why it's a company, not a feature

AI memory — *what a company's AI collectively knows* — is the newest **system
of record**, alongside Salesforce (customer data), GitHub (code), Snowflake
(analytics), Okta (identity). Nobody owns the AI-memory one yet, and every
incumbent is **structurally disqualified** from building it:

- Vendors (Microsoft, Salesforce, OpenAI) can't build the thing that makes
  themselves replaceable — it destroys their lock-in.
- Memory engines (Mem0, Zep, Letta) are *stores*, not neutral rails between
  stores; helping a customer leave Mem0 is against Mem0's interest.
- MemoryLake (China) is another lock-in ("put everything in OUR lake") and is
  disqualified from Western enterprise on geopolitics.

The neutral seat can only be taken by a newcomer. That's the opening.

## Tailwinds (why now)

- **EU Data Act** (in force Sept 2025): vendors must allow switching anytime,
  hand data over in machine-readable format, cooperate with the receiving
  provider; switching fees illegal by 2027. The extraction crowbar.
- **EU AI Act** high-risk obligations (Aug 2026): record-keeping of what
  shaped an AI's decisions → our flight recorder becomes mandatory equipment.
- **Switching wave:** first-gen agent-platform contracts (2024–25) renewing
  now; memory engines already publish "migrate from X" guides.

## The moat (built in layers)

1. **Connector edge-cases** — undocumented per-platform quirks; can't be
   Googled, only earned over many migrations.
2. **Connector network effect** — N systems ⇒ N² routes; a late entrant must
   rebuild the whole matrix.
3. **The canonical format** — everything flows through our schema; publish it,
   make it the standard (the MCP/A2A playbook).
4. **Two-sided flip** — enterprises pay to escape → platforms lose deals over
   "no export path" → platforms certify against us → adversaries become
   distribution.
5. **Compliance** — the answer to "how do we meet Data Act / AI Act for our
   agents?" Auditors name us in checklists.
6. **Neutrality** — the one position no incumbent can take.
7. **Live sync** — once we're the daily interchange, removal = lobotomy.

cold start — becomes a feature launch once everyone is already connected.
